# Public research policy
Define a question by population, exposure/intervention, comparator, outcome and timescale. Record search date, database, exact query and eligibility. Prefer relevant systematic reviews and meta-analyses while appraising quality, overlap, bias and applicability. Controlled human studies may resolve questions reviews do not answer; preclinical mechanisms do not establish human benefit.

Verify title, authors, journal, year and any identifier against the original publication or authoritative record. Record abstract-only access honestly. Never invent a DOI, PMID, trial, sample size, statistic or effect. Unknown means unknown, not zero. Agency guidance and registries do not replace outcome-specific efficacy evidence.

Extract limitations, adverse events, funding, conflicts and uncertainty alongside outcomes. Grade an outcome in a population, not a product in general. Link the exact educational claim to its sources and document review decisions. Distinguish association from causation and narrow task outcomes from everyday or long-term effects.

Use **UNVERIFIED — RESEARCH REQUIRED** for unresolved claims and exclude them from commercial benefit claims. Check quarterly and respond earlier to corrections, retractions, safety alerts or material contradictory findings. Independent scientific review is required before commercial publication; automated checks are supporting tools.
