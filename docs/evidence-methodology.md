# Evidence methodology
Start with a precise question. A useful evidence record names who was studied, what exposure or intervention was compared, the outcome, duration and uncertainty. A conclusion about one task or population does not automatically apply to other users or everyday life.

Search, screen, verify and extract before grading. Review quality matters more than source type alone: systematic reviews can be indirect, dated or overlapping. Note abstract-only access, risk of bias, conflicts, imprecision and inconsistency. Unknown fields remain unknown.

Each card connects a bounded conclusion to verified references, practical relevance, risk, feasibility and what the evidence does not establish. Personal observation tools do not upgrade evidence grades or establish causation.
