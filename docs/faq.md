# Frequently asked questions
**Is this the complete product?** No. It is public methodology, documentation and selected examples. The paid guide, worksheets and tools remain private and unfinished.

**Is the project medical advice?** No. It does not diagnose, treat or cure conditions. Individualized decisions belong with qualified healthcare professionals.

**Does public mean open source?** No. Read [LICENSE](../LICENSE.md). Public visibility does not grant commercial or paid-product redistribution rights.

**Are example observations real customer data?** No. All public examples and screenshots use invented demonstration observations.

**Is A–G a formal clinical grade?** No. It is an editorial rubric and must be accompanied by outcome, population, confidence and limitations.

**Can I run the complete dashboard from this repository?** No. Screenshots document the private prototype; customer runtime builds are not published here.

**Can I submit a correction?** Yes, follow [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md). Avoid personal medical data, secrets and private content.

**When is 1.0 available?** No release date is promised. [The roadmap](../ROADMAP.md) records acceptance gates.
