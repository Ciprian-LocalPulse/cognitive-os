# Research principles
- Verify identifiers and bibliographic metadata at their original source; never create missing facts from memory.
- Prioritize applicable reviews and meta-analyses while assessing bias, overlap and precision.
- Separate human, observational, experimental, mechanistic and preclinical evidence.
- Preserve contrary findings and limits rather than cherry-pick positive results.
- Distinguish a verified source from a verified claim supported by that source.
- Write original educational prose; do not copy protected competitors or full research texts.
- Reassess on corrections, retractions, safety signals and scheduled reviews.
- Keep medical care outside the product's educational scope.

The public preview does not assert that the full evidence library or commercial scientific review is complete.
