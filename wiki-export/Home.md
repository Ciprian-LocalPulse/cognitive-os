# Cognitive OS Wiki

## Mission and reading routes

Cognitive OS is a research-oriented educational and technical framework for organizing evidence about focus, learning, memory and everyday cognitive performance. This Wiki explains its concepts, research method, software architecture and boundaries. Public documentation and technical information about Cognitive OS are maintained in this repository. The Wiki is a reading interface for that documentation, with a source-controlled copy in wiki-export so that changes remain reviewable.

The project attempts to connect questions that are often discussed separately: what research supports, what an educational explanation adds, what a person can reasonably implement, and what their own observations can establish. These questions require different kinds of evidence. A valid source identifier does not guarantee a sound interpretation, and a favorable personal comparison does not validate a framework. The navigation below preserves those distinctions.

## Start with a question

Readers seeking an overview can begin with Introduction to Cognitive OS and the Whitepaper. Researchers can examine the Evidence Framework and Research Methodology before reading domain pages. Developers can start with System Architecture, Local-First Dashboard and Privacy, and Technical Validation and Testing. Readers interested in ordinary reflection can use Self-Observation and Personal Experiments alongside Safety and Ethical Boundaries.

The domain pages are not treatment instructions. Learning and Memory explains distinctions between practice, retrieval and outcomes. Focus, Attention and Deep Work discusses interruptions without prescribing a universal productivity schedule. Sleep, Recovery and Circadian Factors identifies conceptual inputs while avoiding medical sleep treatment. Physical Activity and Cognitive Research separates research domains from claims about individual disease prevention.

## Principles across the Wiki

Evidence transparency means that populations, outcomes and limitations accompany an interpretation. Scientific caution means that mechanisms, correlations and intervention results are not interchangeable. Local-first privacy means that ordinary records are intended to remain under the user's control, while the actual limits of browser storage remain explicit. Educational use means that the interface does not diagnose or direct individualized healthcare decisions.

Structured implementation should help a person make a modest plan and review its feasibility. It should not compel tracking, penalize missing observations or equate productivity with health. Reproducibility concerns traceable documentation and technical artifacts; it cannot replace a study of effectiveness. Explicit limitations remain part of the framework rather than an appendix that can be ignored when making a claim.

## Public resources and status

The [Whitepaper](https://github.com/Ciprian-LocalPulse/cognitive-os/blob/main/WHITEPAPER.md) presents the complete conceptual argument. The [documentation index](https://github.com/Ciprian-LocalPulse/cognitive-os/blob/main/docs/README.md) supplies shorter implementation-oriented reference pages. [Research policy](https://github.com/Ciprian-LocalPulse/cognitive-os/blob/main/RESEARCH_POLICY.md), [architecture](https://github.com/Ciprian-LocalPulse/cognitive-os/blob/main/ARCHITECTURE.md) and [evidence grading](https://github.com/Ciprian-LocalPulse/cognitive-os/blob/main/EVIDENCE_GRADING.md) state project-level commitments. [Security](https://github.com/Ciprian-LocalPulse/cognitive-os/blob/main/SECURITY.md) describes reporting boundaries, and the [roadmap](https://github.com/Ciprian-LocalPulse/cognitive-os/blob/main/ROADMAP.md) distinguishes future questions from implemented work.

This documentation edition is 1.0.0. It is not a clinical validation milestone or an independent peer-review designation. The framework uses selected research coverage and does not claim an exhaustive systematic review. Source examples and screenshots are deliberately bounded illustrations, not participant records. Where a page describes the dashboard, it describes a design rather than a runtime included in this repository.
