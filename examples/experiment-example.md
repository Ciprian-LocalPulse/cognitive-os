# Educational experiment example

**Invented observations, not a real study or personal record.**

Question: what focus ratings do I record during sessions when notifications are silenced?
Metric: self-reported focus, 1–5. Baseline: three observations (2, 3, 3); follow-up: three (3, 4, 3).

The baseline mean is 2.67 and follow-up mean is 3.33. The observed change is +0.67 rating points after rounding. This does not establish causation, clinical improvement, significance or generalizable benefit. Different tasks, workload, expectations and recording habits may explain the pattern.

Plan consistent recording and note what else changed. Missing observations remain missing. This is an educational reflection, not individualized health guidance.
