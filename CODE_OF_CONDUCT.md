# Code of conduct
Discuss evidence, design and documentation respectfully. Welcome differing backgrounds, abilities and experience. Critique claims and reasoning without attacking people. Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, publish another person's private information or pressure others to disclose health conditions.

Keep discussion within educational scope. Do not prescribe treatment to other participants or make unsupported health promises. Do not use project spaces for spam or uninvited commercial promotion.

The maintainer may edit/remove inappropriate content and limit participation when necessary. Use private vulnerability reporting for sensitive reports when available; public issues may request a confidential channel without exposing details. Enforcement should be proportionate, documented and open to a private appeal. This repository is not an emergency or crisis-support channel.
