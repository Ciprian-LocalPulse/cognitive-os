# Public asset descriptions

Branding illustrations are conceptual artwork, not anatomical figures or evidence of efficacy. The hero and social-preview derivatives retain neutral Cognitive OS identity. No new scientific inference is conveyed by a network-shaped illustration.

Dashboard screenshots show the historical 0.1.0-alpha prototype with synthetic observations. They do not depict participant records, current runtime certification or clinical validation. Architecture and evidence-pipeline illustrations describe information organization.

Assets already present under neutral names are retained. No unrelated source assets or deliverable files are copied into this repository. Text descriptions accompany their use in the README; research and architecture figures should also explain the meaning of their arrows.
